# Zelos v1.0 to v1.1: changelog for judges

Generated by `scripts/build_defense_kit.py` from `results/*.json` (results produced at commit `3d224c2` on 2026-10-10). Every v1.1 figure is read from the results files; v1.0 figures are read from `results/results.json` at git tag `v1.0-submission`.

> **v1.0 (tag `v1.0-submission`) is what was submitted and evaluated.** Its write-up (`docs/WRITEUP.md`), limitations (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md`) and numbers are frozen and were not rewritten. **v1.1 is a post-deadline build** on branch `main`, after the tag, prepared for the final round. Where v1.1 contradicts v1.0, this file says so and says which number to trust.

## What was corrected

Each item below is a statement in v1.0 that v1.1 changes, and the number you should use.

### 1. v1.0 evaluated 480 of 3,072 images

The submission used a seeded, stratified subset to stay reproducible on a laptop. Its confidence intervals therefore describe 301 test images. v1.1 evaluates all 3,072 images; the expert-label test set is 2,110 images from 39 sessions (968 expert-bad). Use the v1.1 intervals.

### 2. The pre-registered reference flagged 76.7% of clean test images

In v1.0 this was reported as an honest failure (cross-session acquisition shift) with a post-hoc batch reference that lowered it to 46.7%. v1.1 replaces the single z threshold with per-metric calibrated cutoffs and a session-relative reference. At that operating point 3.3% (session-relative) and 1.7% (single image) of clean images are flagged. The retired v1.0 rule is not hidden: on v1.1's held-out sessions the pre-registered z=3 rule still flags 64.7% (pooled reference), 55.0% (between-session) and 32.4% (batch) of expert-good images. Detection therefore looks lower in v1.1 (63.3% against 97.1%): that is the honest operating point, not a regression. At a threshold of 0.5 the same detector finds 88.1% at 20.0% clean false flags (`threshold_sweep`).

### 3. The artifact score's AUROC of 0.393 on 301 images

v1.0 reported 0.393 [0.332, 0.459] and concluded that the artifact score does not predict the expert label. In v1.1 the score is approximately at chance, with intervals that include chance level: 0.571 [0.493, 0.647] (session-relative), 0.518 [0.416, 0.629] (single image) and 0.491 [0.367, 0.627] (pooled reference); random is 0.487. The qualitative conclusion stands; the number is superseded and the apparent below-chance result should not be quoted.

### 4. Cause attribution of 31.0% among flagged is not comparable with v1.1

The flagged set differs: v1.0 attributed causes for images flagged at a threshold that also flagged 76.7% of clean images, v1.1 for images flagged at a calibrated threshold, so the two accuracies are computed on different images. In v1.1 the accuracy among flagged faults is 85.4% with signatures and 72.6% with the v1.0 rule table (session-relative), 81.2% and 52.3% for single images, against 11.1% by chance. Treat the v1.0 figure as a measurement of v1.0's flagged set only.

## What did not change

- The grounding gate, the retrieval stack and the corpus. E4 and E5 were re-run offline from the committed caches: their headline values are unchanged (100/100 fabrications caught, 0/98 false blocks, unsupported claims 0.067 to 0.000, paper-level MRR 1.000).
- The research-only scope: no clinical or diagnostic claims, certificates are content hashes and not signatures.
- Tag `v1.0-submission`, `docs/WRITEUP.md` and `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.

## New limitations in v1.1

These are in addition to everything in `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.

1. **Single-image absolute mode cannot meet the false-alarm target on the upper fifth of unseen sessions.** Leave-one-session-out mean false-flag rate is 17.6% (pooled reference 21.3%) against a 10% target, and the 80th-percentile session is at 28.4% (pooled 31.1%); session-relative scoring is at 9.7% (80th percentile 13.3%) but needs a batch of images from the same session (4 to 12 in the API).
2. **The benchmarks are small.** 60 base images for the injected-fault benchmark, 20 per group for the vision-LLM baseline, 38 grounding cases (14 adversarial). Intervals are reported and are wide.
3. **Some fault kinds are mostly missed.** At severity 3 (session-relative): debris 3.3% at severity 3; uneven illumination 8.3% at severity 3; motion blur 23.3% at severity 3. Cause naming for these kinds is also weak.
4. **The culture-state kNN uses expert-labelled neighbours.** It is a lookup against a human judgement of unknown criteria, and in the product the bank includes all 3,072 images, so an image from a session already in the bank looks better than one from a new session.
5. **The injected faults are synthetic and ours.** The benchmark shows sensitivity to these faults, not their prevalence or realism.
6. **The head-to-head and the vision-LLM baseline are narrow.** One approach implemented here; one prompt; 2 models with usable replies and one with no parsable reply on 80 cases.
7. **5 of 9 lab actions have no verified literature basis** in the 48-paper corpus, and the 0.50 support threshold was chosen after reading the matches.
8. **The latency figure was measured in the v1.0 run** on 480 images and was not re-measured on all 3,072.
9. **One model in the LLM sweep was not measured**: the Cohere chat model, because the shared trial key reached its monthly quota.

## Post-hoc choices, disclosed

| Choice | When | Where it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| Batch (session-relative) reference | After v1.0's cross-session result | `docs/WRITEUP.md` section 6 (v1.0) |
| Per-metric calibrated cutoffs and signature attribution | After v1.0's failures; fitted on development sessions only | `results/calibration.json`, `scripts/eval_vision_v2.py` |
| Rule-citation threshold 0.30 to 0.50 | After reading the matches | `results/rule_citations.json` → `disclosure` |
| Reasoning-model settings in the LLM sweep | One probe per model | `docs/LLM_SWEEP.md` |

No threshold was tuned on test sessions in v1.1. The designer had seen v1.0's test results before designing v1.1, and the split was redrawn at session level, so a prospective validation with a partner lab remains the only clean test.
