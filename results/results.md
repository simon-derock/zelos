## Findings

- E2 on 2110 images from 39 held-out imaging sessions (of 3072 images, 59 sessions): the artifact QC score reaches AUROC 0.571 against the expert label, DINOv2 kNN (expert-labelled neighbours, no model trained) reaches 0.767, a label-trained linear head 0.803.
- E2 within the same imaging day (no date shortcut): DINOv2 kNN AUROC 0.735 (weighted over 28 sessions); artifact score 0.661.
- E1 (60 real test-session images x 11 faults x 3 severities, batch_session): clean false-flag rate 3.3% at the calibrated threshold (pooled-reference v1: 3.3%); severity>=3 faults detected 63.3%; cause named correctly 85.4% with signature centroids vs 72.6% with the rule table (chance 11.1%).
- Head-to-head on the same injected faults, each at its own <=10% session-averaged false-alarm calibration: the label-trained DINOv2 head flags 5.0% of clean and detects 25.5% of severity>=3 faults (AUROC 0.710) and cannot name a cause; Zelos on a single image detects 66.4% (AUROC 0.882), with a session reference 63.3% (AUROC 0.913), and names the cause.
- Rule basis: 4 of 9 cause-action rules have a verified sentence in the open corpus (threshold 0.5, post hoc); the rest are reported unsupported.
- Vision-LLM baseline (n=20 per group): mistral-small-3.1-24b-instruct: detects 90.0%, false flags 40.0%; llama-4-scout-17b-16e-instruct: detects 100.0%, false flags 65.0%; Zelos detects 80.0%, false flags 0.0%.
- LLM sweep behind the gate: explain_qc: best supported claims per case at zero unsupported after the gate = llama-3.1-8b-fast (6.15/case, n=20). Cheapest within 5% of best = llama-3.1-8b-fast (6.15/case, $0.0182 per 100 answers).
- E4: over 48 open-access papers / 2379 chunks the best mode is hybrid_rerank (paper-level MRR 1.000).
